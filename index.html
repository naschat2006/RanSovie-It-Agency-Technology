Everything You Need to Know About Our Process & Pricing

Clear, transparent answers to our engagement models, milestones, sprint timelines, and pricing guarantees before we write the first line of code.

We execute every engagement using a battle-tested 4-phase agile delivery framework engineered for speed, predictability, and high code quality:

1. Discovery & Strategy (Days 1–5) Stakeholder roadmap alignment, tech stack selection, user journey maps, and measurable milestone definitions.
2. UI/UX Prototyping (Weeks 1–2) High-fidelity Figma tokens, interactive click-through designs, and UX usability sign-offs before coding.
3. Agile Engineering (Weeks 2–6) Production-grade full-stack implementation with weekly staging preview URLs and automated unit tests.
4. QA, Launch & Handover (Final Sprint) 95+ Google Core Web Vitals audit, technical SEO indexing, DNS switch, and complete 100% IP code transfer.

You receive weekly Friday sprint demos and private dashboard access throughout the lifecycle.

We stand for 100% price certainty with transparent, milestone-tied billing. We provide three engagement frameworks:

  • Fixed-Milestone Contracts: Typical deliverables are split into 3 clear payments: 40% on project kickoff & architecture, 30% after interactive UI/UX sign-off, and 30% upon final QA approval prior to live domain release.
  • Standard Monthly Retainers: Choose between our Basic ($499/mo) or Professional ($999/mo) plans for continuous feature development, SEO optimization, and proactive maintenance.
  • Flexible Payment Channels: We accept global wire transfers (USD/EUR/GBP), Stripe international credit cards, and regional direct banking (bKash, Nagad, SSLCommerz) with formal tax invoices.

Every dollar is documented in a formal Statement of Work (SOW) with defined acceptance criteria.

Delivery schedules are determined by architectural scope, but our agile process ensures industry-leading turnarounds:

Brand Identity & Design System 7 – 14 business days
High-Converting Business Web Portal 3 – 5 weeks
Full-Stack SaaS & Custom E-Commerce 6 – 10 weeks

Need rapid go-to-market? Ask about our Expedited Sprint Track for targeted MVPs launched in under 14 days.

Zero vendor lock-in, zero hidden fees. We believe in complete transparency and full client sovereignty:

  • 100% Intellectual Property Ownership: You retain complete legal ownership of all source code, Figma source files, 3D assets, and copyright from day one upon final invoice settlement.
  • Direct Cloud Accounts: Hosting infrastructure (e.g. Vercel, AWS, Google Cloud, Cloudflare) is provisioned directly under your organization's credentials so you pay wholesale hosting costs directly.
  • No Surprise Invoices: Any potential third-party API costs (e.g. Twilio, OpenAI, Google Maps) are scoped and approved in writing prior to integration.

We design collaboratively and maintain an iterative feedback loop so the final outcome surpasses expectations:

  • Included Revisions: Every project package includes up to 3 structured rounds of revisions during both the UI/UX design phase and the staging build phase.
  • Interactive Review Tools: Leave pinpoint visual comments directly inside our shared Figma boards and staging review URLs without endless back-and-forth emails.
  • Mid-Project Scope Changes: If your business requirements evolve, we calculate an itemized change request with exact hours and cost before initiating any non-scoped features.

Our relationship doesn't stop at deployment. We stand firmly behind every system we craft:

30-Day Complimentary Code Warranty

Any unexpected bug, layout discrepancy, or technical glitch identified within 30 days of launch is patched and deployed immediately at zero charge to you.

For teams requiring ongoing SLA uptime guarantees, we offer 24/7 server monitoring, regular dependency security patches, and priority development hours starting at $499/mo.

We never use bloated pre-made templates or slow page-builder plugins. We engineer tailored modern web architectures:

React & Next.js Server-side rendering & instant routing
Sub-Second Speed 95+ Lighthouse Score & Web Vitals
Enterprise Security SSL/TLS, CSRF protection, sanitized inputs

Getting started is straightforward and friction-free:

  1. Initial Consultation: Submit a request via our Contact Page or quote builder with your project goals.
  2. Proposal & Scoping (Within 24 Hours): We prepare an itemized deliverable breakdown, architecture diagram, and fixed quote.
  3. Kickoff Call & Workspace Setup: Upon contract signing, we invite your team to a private Slack or WhatsApp channel, configure your Figma space, and begin Day 1 Discovery within 48 hours.
